# ctf-writeups
A collection of write-ups where I document the Capture The Flag (CTF) challenges I've worked on while learning more about cybersecurity and offensive security.

I use these write-ups to keep track of what I've learned, explain how I approached each challenge, and share the techniques I used to solve it. Some challenges are straightforward, while others take a bit more research, trial and error, and problem-solving.

---

### Categories

The repository covers different areas of cybersecurity, including:
- **Web** – Web vulnerabilities, authentication issues, access control, and application logic.
- **Pwn** – Binary exploitation, memory corruption, and low-level security.
- **Crypto** – Cryptographic weaknesses, encryption, and mathematical challenges.
- **Forensics** – Analysing files, artefacts, and other digital evidence.
- **OSINT** – Open-source intelligence, information gathering, and investigating publicly available data.
- **Misc** – Challenges that don't fit neatly into the other categories.

---

### Write-up Structure

Each write-up is a little different depending on the challenge, but I try to include:
- A brief overview of the challenge.
- Initial analysis and reconnaissance.
- The steps I took to investigate the problem.
- The vulnerability or key concept behind the challenge.
- The approach used to reach the solution.
- Anything new I learned, along with useful references.

The goal isn't just to show the final answer, but also to explain the thought process behind it.
